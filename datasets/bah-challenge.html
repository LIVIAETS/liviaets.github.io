3rd AH Video Recognition Challenge

The 3rd edition of the Ambivalence / Hesitancy (A/H) Video Recognition Challenge, part of the 11th Workshop and Competition on Affective & Behavior Analysis in-the-Wild (ABAW) at ECCV 2026 — built on the BAH dataset.

Register & read the full call → ABAW 11th website

Overview

Participating teams get access to the Behavioural Ambivalence and Hesitancy (BAH) dataset — a video dataset for ambivalence/hesitancy recognition with rich annotations that can be used to develop expression-recognition methods. The challenge is now open for registration, allowing teams to access the BAH training data.

The task

This edition's task is to predict ambivalence/hesitancy at the video level: is there ambivalence/hesitancy in a given video? Teams develop and submit models trained on the BAH data, evaluated on a held-out test set.

Awards & publication

The top 3 winning teams will contribute paper(s) describing their approach, methodology and results to the workshop, in addition to making their code public. The accepted papers will be part of the ECCV 2026 proceedings.

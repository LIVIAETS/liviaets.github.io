3rd AH Video Recognition Challenge

Predict ambivalence and hesitancy at the video level, the 3rd edition of our challenge at the 11th ABAW workshop, built on the BAH dataset.

★ 3rd edition 📅 Test set Jul 10, 2026 🏆 Top 3 in ECCV 2026 proceedings
Register & read the full call → ABAW 11th website

01 · OverviewAbout the challenge

Participating teams get access to the Behavioural Ambivalence and Hesitancy (BAH) dataset, a richly annotated video dataset for ambivalence/hesitancy recognition, to develop and benchmark their own expression-recognition methods.

300participants
1,427videos
3rdchallenge edition
ECCV2026 proceedings

The challenge is now open for registration, allowing teams to access the BAH training data. It is part of the 11th Workshop and Competition on Affective & Behavior Analysis in-the-Wild (ABAW) at ECCV 2026.

02 · The taskWhat you'll build

This edition's task is to predict ambivalence/hesitancy at the video level: is there ambivalence or hesitancy in a given video? Teams train models on the BAH data and submit predictions evaluated on a held-out test set.

03 · AwardsPublication & recognition

The top 3 winning teams will contribute paper(s) describing their approach, methodology and results to the workshop, in addition to making their code public. The accepted papers will be part of the ECCV 2026 proceedings.
